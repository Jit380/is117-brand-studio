01 / Modernist 02 / Postmodern
Design study / references & credits

An interpretation of postmodern quotation and juxtaposition: an Apple-inspired product reveal, a display wordmark competing in scale with the object, and the contrast of geometric Syne with an italic serif. Calm surfaces hold different visual voices. Intended effect: understated expression with emotional depth. Risk: the editorial type could distract from the shirt. Reference: Bonjour Monde & Lucas Descroix, Syne for Synesthésie (2017; open-source release 2018). Fonts used under the SIL Open Font License.

Both studies use the same product image, headline, supporting copy, story and action. EKO is a learner-selected name; this typography is a provisional wordmark. The prose is an AI-drafted interpretation of the learner’s stated meaning. The Apple reference is the learner’s inspiration; the current Apple website could not be inspected in this environment. No music, lyrics, product endorsements or unsupported garment claims are used.
